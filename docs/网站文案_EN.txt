About Robinson

A Lifecycle Approach to Compute Infrastructure

Successful compute infrastructure projects require continuity across investment planning, construction, equipment deployment and ongoing operations.

Robinson aligns resources and services around each project’s requirements, supporting delivery and long-term operation.

Our services connect investment, construction and operations, with server and supporting equipment supply integrated into project delivery. As our business develops, we plan to explore infrastructure asset ownership and long-term management.
